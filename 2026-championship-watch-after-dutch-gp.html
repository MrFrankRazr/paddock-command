CHAMPIONSHIP WATCH · SEP 22

Antonelli Can Clinch the Title as Early as Singapore

Kimi Antonelli arrives in Baku with an 81-point championship advantage — and Formula 1’s first title-clinching permutations show how quickly that margin could become decisive.

Paddock Command editorial · Updated Sep 22, 2026

ANTONELLI292 pts

NEXTBaku · Sep 24–26

The title clock has started

Kimi Antonelli leads George Russell 292–211 with nine rounds remaining. Lewis Hamilton sits third on 191 points.

Formula 1’s first official title permutations show that the earliest mathematical clinch is Singapore, Round 17. That extreme scenario requires Antonelli to win Azerbaijan and Bahrain before sweeping both the Singapore Sprint and Grand Prix, while Russell scores no points across those events.

Championship Watch

Current lead: 81 points.

Mexico is the form-based benchmark

A more representative projection published by Formula 1, based on Antonelli and Russell continuing around their current median finishing positions, points toward Mexico, Round 19, as the earliest likely mathematical conclusion.

That does not make Singapore a prediction. It establishes the mathematical boundary around a championship Antonelli increasingly controls.

Baku begins a potentially decisive sequence

Azerbaijan is the first race in a three-round run through Baku, Bahrain and Singapore that can substantially reshape the remaining title arithmetic.

Antonelli does not need to chase the Singapore scenario. Another substantial points weekend that prevents Russell from cutting the 81-point deficit keeps the championship moving firmly in his direction.

Form remains on Antonelli’s side

Antonelli has won eight of the first 14 Grands Prix, including consecutive victories at Monza and Madrid. Russell finished second at Monza and fifth in Madrid, and has acknowledged that his focus is increasingly race-by-race.

The next championship numbers will come from Baku. Until then, the 81-point gap remains unchanged.
