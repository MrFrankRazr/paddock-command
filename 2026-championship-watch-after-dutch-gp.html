2026 Championship Watch: Antonelli Leaves Monza 66 Points Clear

The weekend that should have exposed the championship leader instead strengthened his control: Antonelli won from 19th while Russell finished second.

Paddock Command editorial · Updated Sep 7, 2026

The headline number is now 66

Kimi Antonelli leads George Russell by 66 points after Monza. Lewis Hamilton is third on 191, Lando Norris fourth on 171, Charles Leclerc fifth on 155 and Max Verstappen sixth on 127.

Antonelli arrived with a 59-point cushion and a back-of-grid penalty; he left with victory and seven more points of separation from Russell.

Monza was the stress test

Russell started second, Hamilton fourth and Antonelli 19th. This was the kind of weekend the chasing group needed to create a large net swing. Instead, Antonelli won.

The chasing group needs larger swings

Russell remains the closest challenger, but simply finishing near Antonelli will not close a 66-point gap. The chase increasingly depends on weekends where a rival wins while Antonelli scores materially fewer points.

Mercedes controls both sides of the equation

Mercedes leaves Monza with 468 constructor points to Ferrari's 346. Its strength gives Antonelli protection in the teams' battle, while Russell remains his closest individual title rival.

Paddock Command title signalTrack the five-race trend, not isolated wins. Antonelli's current sequence combines victories with repeated podiums, making the 66-point advantage harder to attack.

Madrid is the next uncertainty

The inaugural Madrid weekend removes much of the historical circuit reference teams normally carry into a Grand Prix. The championship arrives with Antonelli in a stronger position than before Italy.
