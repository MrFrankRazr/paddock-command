CHAMPIONSHIP WATCH · AFTER ROUND 14

Antonelli Leaves Madrid 81 Points Clear

Back-to-back victories at Monza and Madrid have moved Kimi Antonelli to 292 points and expanded his championship advantage over George Russell to 81.

Paddock Command editorial · Updated Sep 13, 2026

Madrid increases the pressure on every challenger

Antonelli arrived at Madring with a 66-point championship lead. He leaves with 81 after winning the inaugural Spanish Grand Prix while Russell finished fifth.

Antonelli now has 292 points to Russell's 211. Hamilton remains third on 191, Norris is fourth on 186, Leclerc fifth on 167 and Verstappen sixth on 145.

Antonelli's advantage is now performance plus margin

His last five Grand Prix finishes are P1, P3, P2, P1 and P1. That sequence contains three victories and four podiums, including consecutive wins at Monza and Madrid.

Russell needs wins, not only consistency

Madrid demonstrated the problem with simply limiting losses. Fifth place scored 10 points; Antonelli's victory scored 25. A 66-point deficit became 81 in a single afternoon.

Hamilton's retirement compresses the secondary battle

Hamilton remains third on 191 after his brake-related retirement. Norris is now only five points behind on 186, with Leclerc on 167 and Verstappen on 145.

The lead is now 81 points, up 15 from the start of Madrid.

Baku is the next opportunity to interrupt the trend

The Azerbaijan Grand Prix follows on September 24–26. The requirement is increasingly simple: Antonelli's rivals need to start taking points out of him rather than merely staying within range.
