CHAMPIONSHIP WATCH · AFTER BAKU · SEP 26

Russell Cuts Antonelli’s Lead to 66 After Azerbaijan

A 25-point win for Russell and a 10-point recovery for Antonelli change the 2026 championship arithmetic, but the leader still carries a substantial margin into Sepang.

Paddock Command editorial · Updated Sep 26, 2026
Current through the Azerbaijan Grand Prix
ANTONELLI302 pts
RUSSELL236 pts
GAP66 pts
BAKU SWINGRussell +15

Baku changes the margin, not the leader

Antonelli entered Azerbaijan on 292 points to Russell’s 211, an 81-point lead. Russell won from pole for 25 points, while Antonelli recovered from 16th on the grid to fifth for 10. The resulting totals are Antonelli 302 and Russell 236, leaving a 66-point gap.

Why Antonelli’s recovery mattered

Friday’s qualifying crash could have produced a much larger swing. Antonelli’s climb into fifth limited Russell’s net gain to 15 points despite the race win.

What Russell proved

Russell topped both Thursday practice sessions, took pole and won despite two Safety Car restarts and late pressure from Verstappen. The result demonstrates strong Baku form; it does not establish what will happen at the next circuit.

Next checkpoint: Sepang

The Bahrain Grand Prix in Malaysia takes place October 2–4 at Sepang. The next points swing will depend on qualifying, reliability, strategy and race execution at a substantially different circuit.
