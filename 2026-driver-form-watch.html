DRIVER FORM · AFTER BAKU · SEP 26

Russell Wins, Hadjar Returns to the Podium and Antonelli Recovers

Baku produces three distinct form signals: a complete Russell weekend, an immediate Hadjar comeback podium and Antonelli’s recovery drive after qualifying trouble.

Paddock Command editorial · Updated Sep 26, 2026

Russell: complete Baku execution

Fastest in both Thursday sessions, Russell converted his qualifying pole into a race win. The narrow 0.196-second margin over Verstappen makes his late-race defense as relevant as his single-lap speed.

Hadjar: strongest comeback signal

Hadjar returned after missing three races with a wrist injury, qualified fourth and finished third. The result establishes an immediate positive performance signal without assuming it will transfer unchanged to Sepang.

Antonelli: recovery and damage limitation

After his Q1 crash, Antonelli started 16th and finished fifth. Russell gained 15 championship points, but Antonelli retained a 66-point lead.

Verstappen: race pace from eighth

Verstappen progressed from eighth to second and finished just 0.196 seconds behind Russell. His race performance contrasts with a qualifying result outside the front rows.

What to watch next

Sepang’s long corners, heat and different aerodynamic demands will test whether Baku’s signals carry forward. This report reflects observed Azerbaijan results, not a forecast.
