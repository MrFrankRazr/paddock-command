DRIVER FORM · AFTER ROUND 14

Antonelli Turns Momentum Into Control

Back-to-back victories at Monza and Madrid give Kimi Antonelli the strongest current blend of race-winning form and championship position.

Antonelli owns the strongest form signal

Antonelli's last five Grand Prix finishes are P1, P3, P2, P1 and P1: three victories and four podiums, capped by consecutive wins at Monza and Madrid.

The two latest victories came through different routes. Monza required a recovery from the back; Madrid required Antonelli to stay in contention and exploit the decisive Virtual Safety Car pit window.

Russell remains consistent but the conversion gap is growing

Russell's last five sequence is retirement, P7, P3, P2 and P5. The podiums show competitive pace, but Madrid again exposed the difference between scoring well and winning.

Norris remains the closest momentum counterweight

Norris's last five finishes are P7, P1, P1, P4 and P3. Consecutive victories in Hungary and the Netherlands remain one of the season's strongest recent runs, while pole in Madrid showed McLaren's one-lap speed remains potent.

Madrid interrupted the conversion: Norris led early before VSC timing and a slow stop dropped him into recovery mode. Third keeps the podium momentum alive without matching Antonelli's current win rate.

Verstappen adds another podium

Verstappen's second place in Madrid reinforces Red Bull's ability to capitalize when races become strategically complex. He remains well behind Antonelli in the championship but relevant in the immediate podium and victory conversation.

Form signal heading to BakuAntonelli has the clearest combination of ceiling, consistency and championship control. Norris remains the strongest recent counterweight, while Russell needs better conversion and Verstappen continues to maximize opportunities.

Baku tests whether the pattern travels again

The Azerbaijan Grand Prix presents a different profile from Madrid. Antonelli has carried his form across Monza and Madring; Baku is the next test of whether that adaptability continues.
