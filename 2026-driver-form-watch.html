2026 Driver Form Watch: Antonelli Turns Damage Limitation Into Dominance

Monza changed the form picture: the championship leader now owns both the points advantage and the strongest recent podium consistency.

Monza changes the form headline

Before Monza, Lando Norris owned the clearest immediate momentum signal after consecutive victories. Kimi Antonelli still owned the championship. After Italy, Antonelli has both.

His last five Grand Prix finishes are P16, P1, P3, P2 and P1. The Silverstone result is the outlier; the four races since have produced two wins and two additional podiums.

Russell remains consistent but needs conversion

George Russell's last five sequence is P2, retirement, P7, P3 and P2. Monza was another strong podium, but Antonelli's victory meant Russell still lost championship ground.

Norris remains a genuine momentum threat

Norris' last five are P4, P7, P1, P1 and P4. Back-to-back victories remain a strong signal, but fourth at Monza interrupted that run while Antonelli won from 19th.

Form signal heading to MadridAntonelli has the strongest blend of ceiling and floor: repeated podiums plus race wins.

Madrid tests portability

A new circuit is a clean test of whether recent performance travels across track profiles. Qualifying execution, tyre preparation and adaptation become especially important form signals.
