Las Vegas GP Practice: Russell Tops Wet FP2 After Rain Wipes Out FP1 Timing

George Russell set a 1:47.272 on his final lap of Thursday’s wet FP2 in Las Vegas. Pierre Gasly came within 0.071 seconds for Alpine, with Charles Leclerc third for Ferrari. Rain and low grip had limited FP1 to exploratory running with no official lap times.

FP1 offered little running

Drivers who ventured out in the opening session mostly completed installation laps. Ryō Hirakawa and Rafael Câmara were among the first out for Haas, but the low grip left little opportunity to assess the car. FIA FP1 report

Late laps settled FP2

Teams began on full wets and moved to intermediates as grip improved. Lando Norris set the early pace on intermediates before Leclerc and Isack Hadjar took turns at the top. Debris near the final corner brought out a late red flag. After the restart, Russell and Gasly both improved to take the top two spots.

Friday watch

If the track dries, FP3 will give teams their first opportunity this weekend to compare slick-tyre warm-up and dry qualifying pace. On a wet track, watch who can get the intermediates into range quickly and leave enough room for a clear lap.

The FIA stewards imposed a 10-place grid penalty on Oscar Piastri for exceeding his internal combustion engine allocation. His starting position depends on qualifying and the application of grid penalties. FIA stewards’ decision
