LAS VEGAS GRAND PRIX · QUALIFYING INTELLIGENCE · NOVEMBER 20, 2026

Antonelli Fastest in Las Vegas Qualifying as Verstappen Inherits Pole

Kimi Antonelli delivered the fastest lap of Las Vegas Grand Prix qualifying, a 1:33.893, but his existing five-place power-unit penalty changes the race's front-row picture. Max Verstappen qualified second and is listed on pole for Saturday’s race on the published provisional starting grid, ahead of George Russell. Antonelli is listed sixth, with Oscar Piastri 17th after separate penalties.

Antonelli beats Verstappen — but the grid tells another story

Antonelli carried his FP3 form into qualifying and improved to 1:33.893 in Q3. Verstappen's 1:34.151 was 0.258 seconds slower, while Russell qualified third on 1:34.224, only 0.073 seconds behind the Red Bull. Lando Norris took fourth at 1:34.244, just two hundredths behind Russell.

The raw qualifying result is not the starting grid. Antonelli had already received a five-place grid penalty for exceeding his internal combustion engine allocation. Applying that sanction promotes Verstappen to the front of the provisional race-order picture and Russell to the other front-row position; the published provisional grid now lists Verstappen P1, Russell P2 and Antonelli P6. Any later FIA amendment takes precedence.

Ferrari misses Q3 as mid-grid rivals advance

Both Ferraris were eliminated in Q2: Charles Leclerc qualified 11th on 1:35.197 and Lewis Hamilton 12th on 1:35.253. In contrast, Racing Bulls placed both Arvid Lindblad and Liam Lawson into Q3, while Alpine's Pierre Gasly and Franco Colapinto also reached the final segment. That gives the mid-grid fight a different shape to Friday's dry practice order.

What to watch on race night

Verstappen versus Russell: the published provisional front-row pairing puts Red Bull and Mercedes together on the short run to Turn 1. Both demonstrated strong one-lap performance in qualifying, but long-run pace and tyre behaviour remain the decisive unknowns.

Antonelli's recovery: the fastest qualifier must work through traffic instead of starting at the front. Las Vegas rewards straight-line speed and decisive braking, but colder tyres can complicate overtaking attempts after starts and restarts.

Ferrari's recovery: Leclerc is listed P10 and Hamilton P11 on the provisional grid. Both will need race pace and strategy to recover after Q2 exits.

Race: Saturday, November 21, 8:00 p.m. PST · 50 laps around the 6.201 km Las Vegas Strip Circuit. Updated November 21 with the published provisional starting grid. Any subsequent FIA decision or revised grid supersedes this snapshot.
