LAS VEGAS GRAND PRIX · POST-RACE INTELLIGENCE · NOVEMBER 21, 2026

Verstappen Wins Las Vegas Thriller as McLaren Claims Double Podium

Max Verstappen won the 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix over 50 laps, beating Lando Norris by just 0.639 seconds. Oscar Piastri completed a double McLaren podium, with newly crowned world champion Kimi Antonelli fourth and Pierre Gasly fifth.

Verstappen converts pole into a narrow victory

Verstappen started from pole after Antonelli's fastest qualifying lap was offset by a five-place power-unit grid penalty. The Red Bull driver converted that starting advantage into a victory, but the closing margin shows how little separated him from McLaren's leading challenger at the flag.

Norris crossed the line only 0.639 seconds behind Verstappen, with team-mate Piastri another 0.890 seconds back. McLaren therefore placed both cars on the podium in a race where the first three finishers were covered by 1.529 seconds.

Antonelli fourth; Alpine takes fifth and sixth

Antonelli, already confirmed as the 2026 Drivers' World Champion before Las Vegas, finished fourth, 2.574 seconds behind Verstappen. The Mercedes driver had set the fastest qualifying lap on Friday but started sixth after his five-place penalty.

Gasly took fifth and Franco Colapinto sixth for Alpine, a strong double top-six result. Ferrari's Lewis Hamilton finished seventh, while Carlos Sainz was eighth for Williams. George Russell, second on the starting grid, finished ninth ahead of Charles Leclerc in tenth.

Race finishing status

Fernando Alonso was classified 19th despite retiring after 49 laps. Arvid Lindblad and Sergio Pérez were listed as not classified following retirements, while Alexander Albon did not start. The published results list 22 entrants.

Championship watch: do not confuse race order with points totals

The Drivers' and Constructors' Championships were settled before Las Vegas, with Antonelli and Mercedes respectively having secured the titles. Verstappen earned 25 points for victory, Norris 18 and Piastri 15; Antonelli collected 12. The battle for the remaining championship places continues into Qatar and Abu Dhabi.
