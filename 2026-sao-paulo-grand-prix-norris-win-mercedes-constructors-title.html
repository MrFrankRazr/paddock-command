SÃO PAULO GRAND PRIX · POST-RACE DEBRIEF · NOVEMBER 8, 2026

Norris Wins São Paulo Grand Prix as Mercedes Seals Constructors' Championship

Lando Norris mastered a changing Interlagos track to take victory from pole, Max Verstappen charged from 21st on the grid to second, and Mercedes wrapped up the 2026 Constructors’ Championship with both cars in the top five.

GRAND PRIX · 71 LAPS · INTERLAGOS

WinnerLando Norris
PodiumVerstappen · Hadjar
Mercedes2026 Champions

Norris gets the decisive call right

The São Paulo Grand Prix began on a wet track, shifted into a long dry phase and ended with rain returning. Through those changes, Norris kept McLaren in control. The pole-sitter claimed his third victory of 2026, crossing the line 14.602 seconds ahead of Verstappen after 71 laps.

It was not a straightforward lights-to-flag procession. New world champion Kimi Antonelli briefly took the lead during the dry-tyre phase but spun at Turn 1, allowing Norris to move ahead. As changing grip and tyre choices reshaped the order, Norris kept his advantage into the final wet spell.

Verstappen's recovery powers a Red Bull double podium

Verstappen arrived on the grid in 21st after power-unit penalties and climbed all the way to second. His charge was one of the race's defining performances, while team-mate Isack Hadjar completed a double podium for Red Bull in third, 24.485 seconds behind Norris.

Oscar Piastri took sixth for McLaren and Liam Lawson eighth for Racing Bulls. Charles Leclerc moved ahead of Lawson late on to claim seventh, while Lewis Hamilton recovered from 20th on the grid to ninth in his 400th Formula 1 start. Nico Hülkenberg completed the points scorers in tenth for Audi.

Mercedes completes the 2026 championship double

George Russell finished fourth and Antonelli fifth, an outcome sufficient for Mercedes to clinch the 2026 Constructors' World Championship. It is the team's ninth Constructors' title and its first since 2021.

Antonelli secured the Drivers' Championship one week earlier in Mexico City. The two titles therefore belong to Mercedes and its lead driver before the season's final three rounds. Ferrari's São Paulo result — Leclerc seventh and Hamilton ninth — was not enough to keep the teams' contest alive.

For Mercedes, the title came despite a complicated race for Antonelli, who experienced spins in demanding conditions. Russell's progress from seventh on the grid to fourth helped the team secure the necessary result.

Championship Watch and what comes next

With the Constructors' title decided at Interlagos and Antonelli already crowned Drivers' Champion, the remaining rounds turn to the battles for championship positions and the form of Norris, Verstappen and the two Mercedes drivers.

Norris has now won three Grands Prix in 2026, with the latest victory underlining McLaren's pace across both wet and dry conditions. Verstappen's charge from the rear and Hadjar's podium keep Red Bull firmly in the spotlight as the championship moves toward Las Vegas, Qatar and Abu Dhabi.
