2027 F1 Grid Complete: Bearman Re-Signs With Haas

Haas has confirmed Ollie Bearman for a third consecutive season alongside Rafael Câmara. All 22 race seats for the 2027 Formula 1 season are now confirmed. The announcement arrives before the penultimate round in Qatar and closes the remaining uncertainty about next season’s starting field.

Haas confirms its final 2027 seat

The team announced on November 26 that Bearman will remain for 2027, joining fellow Ferrari junior Rafael Câmara. Bearman, 21, has accumulated 71 career championship points across 48 starts according to Haas. He finished fourth in Mexico City in 2025 and fifth in China in 2026.

Câmara’s multi-year agreement was announced in October, and he has already made his Formula 1 race debut in São Paulo. The new confirmation settles the other side of the garage. For the background to the change, see our earlier Haas driver-market report.

Paddock Command analysis: continuity is the starting point

Haas can now plan its winter development around a driver familiar with the current car and a teammate gaining experience before his first full season. Consistent feedback from both sides of the garage could help the engineers compare setup changes and evaluate upgrades. It does not guarantee a rise in the competitive order; that depends on the car, reliability and execution on track.

Across the grid, retaining established partnerships reduces the number of variables teams need to manage as they develop their second generation of cars under the 2026 technical framework. Haas is the exception relative to the lineups that began 2026: Câmara takes the seat previously occupied by Esteban Ocon.

The immediate priority is still Qatar

Haas arrives at Lusail seventh in the 2026 Constructors’ Championship on 31 points, with Audi eighth on 28. The three-point gap gives the team an immediate sporting objective before the winter break. Both championships at the front are already decided, but the remaining positional battles are not. Qatar takes place November 27–29.

Bottom line: The 2027 field is confirmed, while the final two races of 2026 remain to be contested. A settled contract is a planning advantage, not a prediction of the next race result.
