# Paddock Command Editorial Workflow

## Race-weekend publishing rhythm

1. **Tuesday/Wednesday — Championship Watch**
   - Update championship gaps and five-race trends.
   - Link to Standings, Trends, Scenario and Compare.
2. **Thursday — Race Weekend Preview**
   - Confirm the official weekend schedule and circuit facts.
   - Identify 3–5 questions specific to the venue and current title context.
   - Link to Live Center, Circuit Intelligence, Form and Fan Picks.
3. **Friday/Saturday — Driver Form / qualifying context**
   - Update only when there is a meaningful story. Avoid publishing thin session recaps.
4. **Sunday/Monday — Post-Race Debrief**
   - Explain what changed in the race and championship rather than reproducing the classification.
   - Link to Winners, Standings, Trends and next-race preview.
5. **Evergreen — Circuit Guides / data explainers**
   - Refresh when track records, configuration or site features materially change.

## Editorial standards

- Paddock Command analysis must add interpretation; do not republish source articles.
- Verify current facts against primary/authoritative sources before publication.
- Clearly distinguish Paddock Command analysis from official timing, rulings or classifications.
- Use the independent-site disclaimer on every editorial page.
- Add an Article JSON-LD block, canonical URL, unique title and meta description.
- Add each published article to `/articles/index.html` and `/sitemap.xml`.
- Prefer 600–1,200 useful words over short SEO filler.
- Link each story to 2–4 relevant interactive Paddock Command tools.

## Suggested naming

- `YYYY-italian-grand-prix-preview.html`
- `YYYY-championship-watch-after-<race>.html`
- `YYYY-<race>-debrief.html`
- `<circuit>-circuit-guide.html`
- `YYYY-driver-form-watch.html`

## Analytics

Monitor article landing pages in Cloudflare Web Analytics and track outbound/internal engagement with Zaraz. Useful growth KPIs: article → Live Center, article → Standings, article → Fan Picks, article → Compare, and returning visitors around race weekends.


## Commercial independence

- Editorial conclusions are written independently of affiliate, advertising or sponsorship relationships.
- Never alter statistics, rankings or race analysis to favor a commercial partner.
- Keep affiliate disclosures adjacent to commercial recommendation modules.
- Paid/sponsored content must be labeled before the reader enters the sponsored section.
- Source/reference links are not affiliate links unless they are explicitly labeled and configured through the monetization layer.
