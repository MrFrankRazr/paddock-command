AUSTIN FRIDAY PRACTICE · OCT 23

Leclerc Tops Both Austin Sessions — Antonelli Just 0.042s Back

Charles Leclerc led both Friday practice sessions at Circuit of the Americas, setting a 1:35.291 in FP2. Championship leader Kimi Antonelli finished second, just 0.042 seconds behind, with Max Verstappen third at +0.189s. Lewis Hamilton and George Russell completed the top five.

McLaren finished seventh and eighth in FP2 as Norris and Piastri continued to work through handling concerns. Friday’s lap times do not establish the final qualifying order or race pace. Antonelli retains his 102-point championship lead heading into Saturday.

Next sessions: FP3 Saturday 12:30 PM CDT (10:30 AM PT) · Qualifying Saturday 4:00 PM CDT (2:00 PM PT) · Grand Prix Sunday 3:00 PM CDT (1:00 PM PT).

Ferrari’s revised aerodynamic package ran during Friday practice, but the fastest lap alone cannot isolate the upgrade’s performance gain.
