# Paddock Command SEO & Growth Playbook — v2.7.0

## Objective
Grow qualified organic and social traffic, convert more visitors into returning readers/newsletter subscribers, and measure which content and tools create durable audience value.

## Search priorities
1. Race-intent pages: current Grand Prix preview, race weekend center, circuit guide, calendar.
2. Championship-intent pages: standings, driver form, trends, scenario calculator.
3. Evergreen explainers: standings, driver form, race-weekend data.
4. Entity expansion: future driver, constructor and circuit landing pages with unique crawlable content.

## Editorial SEO checklist
- One descriptive H1 and one unique title/meta description.
- Canonical URL points to the published page.
- Article schema has headline, dates, publisher, image and mainEntityOfPage.
- Link to at least 2 relevant Paddock Command tools/pages and 1 related article when natural.
- Update dateModified after substantive editorial changes.
- Add the URL to sitemap.xml and feed.xml when appropriate.
- Use factual, specific headlines; avoid keyword stuffing.

## Internal linking model
Preview → Race Weekend Center + Circuit Guide + Standings + Fan Picks.
Debrief → Standings + Form + Trends + next Championship Watch.
Championship Watch → Standings + Scenario + Compare + Form.
Circuit Guide → Calendar + Race Weekend Center + relevant Preview.
Evergreen guides → the interactive tool they explain.

## Social attribution
Use UTMs on social links. Example:
`?utm_source=x&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=italian_gp_2026&utm_content=preview`
Sources: x, instagram, facebook, youtube. Medium: social. Campaign: stable event/topic slug. Content: preview, debrief, form, fan-picks, circuit-guide, etc.
The site emits `acquisition_landing` once per session when UTM parameters are present.

## Growth events
- acquisition_landing — attributed campaign landing
- internal_link_click — navigation between Paddock Command content/pages
- newsletter_impression / newsletter_cta_click / newsletter_signup_success
- affiliate_click — commercial click

## Weekly review
Review: search clicks/impressions/CTR, landing pages, newsletter conversions, social campaign landings, editorial internal-link clicks, affiliate clicks. Expand pages earning impressions but low CTR; improve pages earning traffic but low downstream engagement.

## 30-day content rule
Do not chase volume. Publish around race intent, championship changes and evergreen questions. Refresh high-impression pages before creating low-value near-duplicates.
