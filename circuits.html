Where source data allows, Paddock Command derives approximate circuit length and race distance from published lap data.

What is derived data?

Jolpica does not provide every official circuit specification. Paddock Command therefore labels derived length and distance estimates explicitly. These calculations are useful context, not replacements for official FIA circuit specifications.
