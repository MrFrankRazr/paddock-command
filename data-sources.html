TRANSPARENCY

Data Sources

Paddock Command separates live/published data sources from calculations performed in the browser.

Championship and race data

Standings, schedules, results, qualifying, sprint and circuit references are primarily sourced from the Jolpica F1 API, an Ergast-compatible public motorsport data service.

Weather

Race-weekend weather context is requested from Open-Meteo using circuit coordinates.

News

The News view links visitors to original publishers. Headlines may be obtained from public news feeds and are presented as links rather than republished articles.

Derived values

Some charts, streaks, estimates, scenarios and circuit metrics are calculated by Paddock Command from published source data. Derived values are labeled where appropriate and should not be treated as official FIA timing, telemetry or regulations.

Paddock Command is an unofficial, independent motorsport fan site and is not affiliated with Formula 1, the FIA, teams, drivers, or their rights holders.
