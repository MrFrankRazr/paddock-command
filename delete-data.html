Privacy & Data Controls

Request Data Deletion

Paddock Command does not require a user account. If you use mobile push notifications, limited device-registration and notification-preference information may be stored by Paddock Command so those notifications can be delivered.

Effective date: September 7, 2026

You can request deletion of Paddock Command mobile notification-registration data at any time.

What Data Can Be Deleted

Depending on your use of the Paddock Command mobile app, a deletion request may apply to:

  • your Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registration token;
  • the randomly generated Paddock Command app/device identifier associated with that registration;
  • your stored notification preferences, such as session reminders, intelligence alerts, weather alerts, results alerts, and reminder lead time;
  • related backend registration, delivery-status, and operational records that can reasonably be associated with that mobile registration.

How to Request Deletion

Send an email to Paddock Command support with the subject line Paddock Command Data Deletion Request.

  1. State that you are requesting deletion of your Paddock Command mobile app data.
  2. If possible, include enough non-sensitive context for us to identify the relevant mobile registration. Do not send passwords, private keys, payment information, or other sensitive credentials.
  3. We may ask for limited additional information if needed to identify the registration associated with your request.

Email a Data Deletion Request

What Happens After a Request

After we can reasonably identify the applicable Paddock Command mobile registration, we will delete or deactivate the associated notification-registration data from systems under our control, subject to any limited retention that is necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or system integrity.

Information retained for those limited purposes will not be used to continue ordinary push-notification service after the applicable registration has been deleted or deactivated.

Local Data on Your Device

Paddock Command may also store preferences, saved articles, cached editorial/data content, and notification state locally on your device. You can remove local app data by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

No User Account Is Required

Paddock Command does not currently provide or require user accounts. This deletion page is therefore focused on mobile notification-registration and related operational data rather than account-profile data.
