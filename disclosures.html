Advertising & Affiliate Disclosures

Paddock Command may be monetized through advertising, sponsorships and affiliate relationships while keeping editorial and statistical presentation independent.

Current monetization status

Paddock Command currently participates in the Expedia Group Travel Creator affiliate program. Selected race-weekend travel links may generate a commission for Paddock Command when a visitor completes a qualifying booking. Paid display advertising is not currently active.

Current affiliate partner

Expedia is used for selected race-weekend travel and accommodation recommendations. Expedia affiliate links are clearly labeled near the recommendation and are tracked separately from ordinary editorial or source links.

Affiliate links

If an outbound link can generate a commission for Paddock Command, a clear disclosure will appear with or immediately before the commercial recommendation. The link itself will also be treated as a sponsored link. A commission does not increase the price paid by the visitor unless a merchant states otherwise.

Sponsored content

Paid or sponsored placements will be clearly labeled. Sponsorship does not grant a sponsor control over championship data, calculated statistics, race results or independent editorial conclusions.

Formula 1 independence

Commercial relationships on this site do not imply endorsement by Formula 1, the FIA, any team, driver, circuit, promoter or rights holder.

Paddock Command is an unofficial, independent motorsport fan site and is not affiliated with Formula 1, the FIA, teams, drivers, or their rights holders.
