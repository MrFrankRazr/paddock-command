Ocon to Leave Haas: 2027 Seat Opens as F1 Driver Market Tightens

Esteban Ocon and Haas have confirmed they will part ways after the 2026 season. Ocon wants to remain in Formula 1, while Haas has yet to announce who will take the available 2027 seat.

Haas confirms the split

Haas confirmed on September 30 that Ocon will leave at the end of the current campaign, closing a two-season spell that began in 2025. Team principal Ayao Komatsu thanked Ocon for his professionalism and his input into development of the VF-26 during the 2026 rules transition. The team says its focus remains on extracting the best possible results together through the final race.

Ocon is targeting another F1 opportunity

Speaking before the Bahrain Grand Prix in Malaysia, Ocon made clear that his priority remains racing in Formula 1. He has also indicated that a temporary reserve role is an option if a race seat does not materialize. Ocon previously served as a Mercedes reserve driver in 2019 before returning to the grid.

What changes for the 2027 grid?

The departure creates a confirmed opening at Haas, but the team has not formally announced Ocon's replacement. Oliver Bearman is expected to continue according to Formula 1 reporting; that expectation is not yet a formal Haas lineup announcement. Ferrari-backed Rafael Câmara and reigning Formula 2 champion Leonardo Fornaroli have been discussed as possible candidates, but neither is a confirmed signing.

Why the timing matters

Haas enters the Sepang weekend with its future driver lineup in the spotlight. Ocon, meanwhile, has the remaining 2026 races to strengthen his case for another opportunity. His immediate challenge is to deliver on track while discussions about the remaining 2027 openings continue.

The broader driver-market picture

Fernando Alonso's confirmed extension at Aston Martin has settled one major question for 2027. Ocon's departure opens another. Paddock Command will separate official team announcements from reported candidates as the market develops.
