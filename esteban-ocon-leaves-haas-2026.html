Ocon to Leave Haas After 2026 as Another 2027 Seat Opens

Esteban Ocon and Haas have confirmed they will separate at the end of the 2026 season, opening another seat on the 2027 Formula 1 grid while Ocon insists his F1 career is not over.

Paddock Command editorial · Published Sep 30, 2026
Confirmed driver-market development

Haas and Ocon confirm the split

The 2027 Formula 1 driver market has another confirmed vacancy. Esteban Ocon will leave Haas at the conclusion of the 2026 season, ending a two-year partnership between the French driver and the American team.

Ocon joined Haas from Alpine for 2025. The team confirmed his departure on September 30 ahead of the Bahrain Grand Prix in Malaysia at Sepang.

Confirmed Ocon will leave Haas after 2026. Haas has not announced his replacement.

Ocon intends to stay in Formula 1

The departure should not be interpreted as Ocon stepping away from Formula 1. He says he remains motivated and hungry to continue, describing the Haas split as “absolutely not the end” of his Formula 1 career.

Ocon enters Sepang 16th in the Drivers’ Championship on seven points, 13 behind team mate Oliver Bearman. Haas is seventh in the Teams’ Championship after 15 Grands Prix.

Why Haas is moving on

Formula 1’s analysis reports that Haas evaluated several young drivers through testing as it considered its 2027 direction. Team principal Ayao Komatsu had already indicated in Baku that the team expected to settle its line-up in the coming weeks.

Rafael Câmara, Leonardo Fornaroli, Jack Doohan and Ryo Hirakawa have all been part of that evaluation picture. Reports place Ferrari junior Câmara prominently among the candidates, but Haas has not confirmed Ocon’s successor.

Driver-market watch Treat every potential replacement as candidate intelligence until Haas makes an official announcement.

Ocon still has races to make his case

The immediate task remains the 2026 season. Ocon scored eighth in Azerbaijan, his first points since Monaco, and says he will continue pushing through the remaining races while pursuing a route back onto the 2027 grid.

With many seats already committed, his options are narrowing. A race seat remains his stated target, while a reserve role could become an alternative if no full-time opening materializes.

Paddock Command Take

The important development is not a rumoured replacement. It is that Ocon’s Haas exit is now official, creating another confirmed opening in the 2027 driver market.

Haas can now make its next move on its own timetable. Ocon, meanwhile, enters the final phase of 2026 needing strong performances while his next destination remains unresolved.

Sepang therefore begins with two parallel stories for the Frenchman: delivering for Haas now, and demonstrating that he belongs somewhere else on the 2027 grid.
