Hadjar Returns With His Red Bull Future Secured

Isack Hadjar returns to Formula 1 in Baku after three races out with a wrist injury — and Red Bull has removed another uncertainty by confirming him alongside Max Verstappen for 2027.

Paddock Command editorial · Published Sep 23, 2026
Azerbaijan GP · Sep 24–26
RETURNAzerbaijan GP
ABSENCE3 Grands Prix
2027Red Bull confirmed
TEAM-MATEMax Verstappen

Red Bull settles Hadjar's immediate and longer-term future

Isack Hadjar's Baku comeback now carries more significance than a simple return from injury. Red Bull has confirmed that the Frenchman will remain with the senior team for the 2027 Formula 1 season alongside Max Verstappen.

The announcement arrives one day before Hadjar gets back into the RB22 at the Azerbaijan Grand Prix. He missed the Netherlands, Italy and Spain rounds after sustaining a small fracture to the outside of his wrist during summer training.

Paddock Command watch pointThursday should be judged on more than outright lap time. Hadjar's braking consistency, ability to complete both sessions and comfort over sustained running will provide the clearest early evidence of how complete his recovery is.

Hadjar says the wrist is ready — but not pain-free

Hadjar says simulator running went well and that the wrist is now strong enough to handle the loads expected in the cockpit. He has also acknowledged that some pain is likely to remain for a while.

That distinction matters at Baku. The street circuit combines repeated heavy braking, rapid changes of direction and barriers that leave almost no margin for a loss of confidence or precision. Hadjar has not raced since the Hungarian Grand Prix, so Thursday's FP1 and FP2 sessions will also require him to rebuild a live-track rhythm quickly.

Why Red Bull has committed for 2027

Before the injury, Hadjar had established a consistent scoring run in his first season with Red Bull. Formula 1's official analysis notes seven consecutive top-six finishes between Miami and Hungary, including a Monaco podium, and places him eighth in the championship on 71 points entering Baku.

Red Bull's decision gives the senior team continuity into 2027 and removes one major variable from next season's driver market. Verstappen and Hadjar will remain the team's pairing while Red Bull continues developing under the current technical regulations.

Lawson returns to Racing Bulls

Liam Lawson returns to Racing Bulls after three substitute appearances for Red Bull. His temporary promotion gave the team continuity while Hadjar recovered, but the Azerbaijan weekend restores Red Bull's regular Verstappen-Hadjar line-up.

For Hadjar, the contract means Baku is not an audition for 2027. The immediate task is simpler: re-establish race-weekend rhythm, validate the wrist under real Formula 1 loads and resume the performance trajectory that earned the extension.

Thursday becomes the first validation point

Baku's unusual Thursday-to-Saturday schedule accelerates the story. FP1 begins Thursday at 12:30 local time and FP2 follows at 16:00. There is little time for a cautious return before qualifying preparation becomes important.

Paddock Command will track Hadjar's session completion, braking stability, long-run consistency and qualifying preparation against Verstappen. Those indicators should say more about the quality of the comeback than any isolated practice position.
