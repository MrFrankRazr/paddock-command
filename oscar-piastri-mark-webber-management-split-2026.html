Piastri and Webber End Management Partnership as McLaren Driver Restructures Team

Oscar Piastri and his long-standing management team led by Mark and Ann Webber have mutually agreed to end their partnership. The McLaren driver will operate under a new management structure.

CONFIRMED · FORMULA 1 ANNOUNCEMENT

The separation concerns Piastri's representation, not a confirmed change of team or Formula 1 race seat. The joint announcement was reported by Formula 1 on Monday, November 9.

A partnership that helped launch an F1 career

The Webbers began working with Piastri in 2019, when he won the Formula Renault Eurocup and joined Renault's junior programme. Piastri went on to win the Formula 3 championship in 2020 and Formula 2 title in 2021, placing him on the verge of a Formula 1 debut.

Mark Webber played a prominent role in securing Piastri's move from the Alpine programme to McLaren for 2023. That decision followed the high-profile dispute over which team held the driver's contract. Piastri made his Grand Prix debut with McLaren and established himself as a race winner.

Why the agreement ended

According to the joint statement, the parties mutually agreed that it was the appropriate time to conclude their management arrangement. The Webbers had already reduced their regular trackside involvement during the 2026 season.

Piastri publicly thanked Mark and Ann Webber for their guidance, particularly during the beginning of his Formula 1 career. Webber credited the work the partnership had done in helping Piastri reach the grid and compete at the front, while expressing support for the driver's new management structure.

Formula 1 reported that former Prema race engineer Pedro Matos and mental coach Emma Murray had assumed larger roles within Piastri's immediate trackside team this year. The announcement did not identify a complete replacement management roster.

No confirmed McLaren departure

The change concerns commercial and professional management rather than a confirmed move to another Formula 1 constructor. Neither the announcement nor the official Formula 1 report says that Piastri is leaving McLaren. Any connection to a future race-seat change would be speculative without a separate confirmation.

That distinction matters after a difficult phase of Piastri's 2026 campaign. He finished sixth in the São Paulo Grand Prix on November 8, while team-mate Lando Norris took victory. Piastri sits seventh in the Drivers' Championship following the race.

What to watch before Las Vegas

The timing places Piastri's management transition in the final stretch of the 2026 season. The next Grand Prix is Las Vegas, November 19–21, followed by Qatar and Abu Dhabi.

The immediate questions are who will formally lead his new management operation and whether additional details are announced. The on-track focus remains Piastri's performance through the closing rounds, not any unverified contract implications.
