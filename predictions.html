Fan Picks scoring

Paddock Command awards three points for an exact podium position and one point when a predicted driver reaches the podium in a different position. A perfect podium scores nine points.

Privacy by design

Fan Picks are stored in local browser storage. Paddock Command does not require an account or upload the prediction history to a central database.
