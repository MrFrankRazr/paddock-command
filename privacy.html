PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective: August 27, 2026

Paddock Command is designed to work without requiring a user account. Favorites, Fan Picks and similar preferences are stored locally in your browser unless a future feature explicitly states otherwise.

Information stored on your device

The site uses browser local storage and caches for favorites, predictions, selected data caches and progressive-web-app files. Clearing site data in your browser removes these items.

Third-party services

When the site requests race data, weather or news, your browser may connect to third-party data providers. Those providers may receive ordinary connection information such as IP address and browser headers under their own policies.

Analytics and advertising

Paddock Command uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure aggregate site traffic and performance. The application also emits non-personal engagement events for features such as navigation, comparisons, scenarios, Fan Picks and favorites when Cloudflare Zaraz event collection is enabled. Paddock Command does not intentionally include names, email addresses or account identifiers in these events. Paddock Command uses disclosed affiliate links in selected contextual modules and may add advertising in the future. Commercial clicks are measured separately from ordinary editorial/source links.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to privacy@paddockcommand.com.

Paddock Command is an unofficial, independent motorsport fan site and is not affiliated with Formula 1, the FIA, teams, drivers, or their rights holders.
