Russell Returns to Baku Specification — Antonelli Keeps Singapore Upgrade

Mercedes will compare two aerodynamic packages during Singapore's Sprint weekend. Russell returns to the previous specification that delivered victory in Azerbaijan, while Antonelli continues with the updated Sepang package and a new front wing.

MCLAREN PERFORMANCE WATCH · OCT 8

McLaren Puts Both Cars in Sprint Qualifying Top Five

SINGAPORE CONDITIONS & TECHNICAL WATCH · OCT 8

FIA Activates Heat Hazard and Confirms Wet-Weather Software Contingency

The FIA has completed its investigation into the Sepang power-unit software malfunction. Revised wet-weather configurations will be reinstated for Singapore if similar wet-weather conditions are expected.

Heat-hazard regulations are active for both the Sprint and Grand Prix. Singapore's unhealthy haze remains under observation, with contingency arrangements available if conditions worsen.

RACING STATUS: Published timetable unchanged.

FRIDAY: FP1 16:30 · Sprint Qualifying 20:30

PADDOCK COMMAND WATCH: Power-unit reliability, cooling requirements, haze advisories and FIA operational guidance.

FIA TECHNICAL WATCH · OCT 9

Race Control Error Affected SQ1 Power Availability

The FIA acknowledges Overtake Mode was not available as intended after the red flag and was briefly reactivated for 27 seconds. Some cars were affected by timing and track position. SQ2 and SQ3 were unaffected by this error. No revised Sprint classification has been verified.
