Racing Bulls Retains Lawson and Lindblad for 2027 as Driver Market Narrows

November 12, 2026Paddock Command EditorialConfirmed team announcement

Racing Bulls will retain Liam Lawson and Arvid Lindblad for the 2027 Formula 1 season, bringing an end to one of the final unresolved driver-pairing decisions on the grid.

The decision follows a strong run of results for Lawson and an encouraging rookie campaign for Lindblad. With three race weekends left in 2026, the team can now focus on the fight for fifth place in the Constructors’ Championship without uncertainty over its next driver lineup.

Lawson’s performances strengthen his position

Lawson has made a compelling case for continuity during the second half of 2026. He finished seventh at the São Paulo Grand Prix on November 8, adding another points result to a season in which he has regularly led the midfield charge.

He sits ninth in the Drivers’ Championship on 89 points, including points scored during his three-race stint substituting for Isack Hadjar at Red Bull. His recovery from earlier career uncertainty has given Racing Bulls an experienced reference point alongside a developing rookie.

Lindblad rewarded after his rookie campaign

Lindblad, in his first full Formula 1 season, has scored 38 points and demonstrated sufficient pace and consistency to earn another opportunity with the Faenza-based team. Retaining the 2026 pairing avoids another reset as Racing Bulls prepares for 2027.

Team principal Alan Permane identified the combination of experience and ambition as a reason for keeping the pair together. Both drivers can carry established engineering relationships and a season of shared feedback into the next campaign.

What the decision means for Nikola Tsolov

Red Bull junior Nikola Tsolov had been considered a contender for a Racing Bulls race seat. With both seats occupied, the Bulgarian will instead take on reserve-driver duties across Red Bull’s Formula 1 teams, according to Reuters. The role is expected to involve simulator development, testing and selected practice opportunities as the organization evaluates his longer-term prospects.

That keeps Tsolov in the development pipeline without requiring an immediate race promotion.

The midfield fight continues in Las Vegas

Racing Bulls is fifth in the Constructors’ standings as the season heads to Las Vegas on November 19–21, followed by Qatar and Abu Dhabi. Confirming the 2027 pairing does not settle the current competition: Lawson and Lindblad still have three events to help protect the team's position.

The decision also narrows the remaining 2027 driver-market questions, including the final makeup of Haas’s lineup. Paddock Command will continue to distinguish formally confirmed seats from speculation as the grid takes shape.
