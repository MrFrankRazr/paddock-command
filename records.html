Record data and reliability

The all-time leaderboards use a maintained records snapshot for reliability, while selected-season milestones remain connected to the live championship data service. This avoids large historical request bursts that can be unreliable in a browser-only application.

Records need context

Formula 1 has changed substantially across eras: calendars, scoring systems, qualifying formats and season lengths are not constant. Paddock Command presents totals as records while leaving era comparisons to the reader rather than pretending every season was directly equivalent.
