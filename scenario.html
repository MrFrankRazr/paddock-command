A projection, not an official table

The Scenario Calculator is designed for fan exploration. It models points against the selected standings, but historical championships can include dropped scores or special rules that make exact retrospective simulation more complicated.

Use it during a title fight

The calculator is most useful late in a season when a few positions can materially change the championship order. It complements the Head-to-Head and Trends tools by turning “what if?” questions into visible point projections.
