How Paddock Command reads the standings

The live command center pulls published championship standings and presents them alongside race results, wins and trend data. Historical seasons use the same season selector, allowing the standings pages to act as both a current championship view and an archive.

Drivers and constructors are different championships

The Drivers’ Championship scores individual competitors. The Constructors’ Championship combines points earned by a team’s eligible cars. Paddock Command keeps both views separate so a driver battle and a team battle can be followed independently.
